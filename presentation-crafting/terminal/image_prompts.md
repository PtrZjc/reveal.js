# Style

* **Core Aesthetic**: High-fantasy digital painting reminiscent of dark action RPGs. The tone is gritty, ancient, and heavy, emphasizing physical weight and arcane energy.
* **Color Palette**: Dominant background tones are desaturated and dark (obsidian black, slate grey, dark umber). Focal points require highly saturated, luminous accent colors (electric amber, glowing cyan, deep crimson, arcane purple).
* **Lighting and Shading**: Chiaroscuro with high contrast between deep shadows and intensely bright light sources. Lighting must be volumetric, originating from in-scene elements like molten metal, glowing runes, or magical fluids.
* **Texture and Rendering**: Emulate thick oil painting with visible digital brushwork. Avoid smooth gradients or vector-like crispness. Surfaces must show wear, age, and tactile properties (hammered iron, cracked stone, scratched glass).
* **Atmosphere and VFX**: Environments must feel dense. Integrate ambient particulate matter such as floating embers, thick smoke, low-hanging fog, or glowing magical dust interacting with light sources.
* **Linework**: No visible outlines or line art. Forms must be defined entirely by light, shadow, and color value boundaries.
* **Perspective and Depth**: Cinematic or isometric angles. Use shallow depth of field to blur distant background elements, keeping the primary subjects sharply in focus.
* **Typography and Text Integration**:
  * **In-World Text (Embedded)**: Text integrated into the scene must feel physically rendered within the environment. Render text as deep chisel-carved stone lettering, hot-stamped branding on forged metal, or glowing arcane runes etched into glass or stone. It must take on the surrounding object's lighting, texture, cast shadows, and volumetric glow rather than appearing pasted on top.
  * **Overlay and Label Text (UI/Callouts)**: Non-diegetic labels, headers, and UI text must use classical serif, chiselled Roman, or refined gothic typefaces with medium-to-heavy weight for high legibility.
